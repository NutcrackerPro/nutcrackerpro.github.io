How do I publish my changes?
  1. Fill in this form. Then choose Copy for GitHub.
  2. Open the GitHub editor link above and sign in to your account.
  3. Replace the file's text with your copied changes, then choose Commit changes. Your site updates after GitHub Pages finishes publishing.

Alternatively, download portfolio.json and upload it to the repository, replacing the existing file. Keep a downloaded copy before leaving this page. Drafts are not automatically saved.

To open this editor from your portfolio, tap the penguin 10 times, leaving no more than 2 seconds between taps. This arms the secret sequence and locks section navigation. Then press ArrowUp ArrowUp ArrowDown ArrowDown ArrowLeft ArrowRight ArrowLeft ArrowRight b a b a (Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right, B, A, B, A). Tap the penguin again to cancel. You can also visit admin.html?v=20260928-secret2 directly. The sequence hides the public entry point; it is not authentication. Anyone can open this draft editor. GitHub checks your account permissions before allowing changes to your actual website.

Choose Refresh published content to load the latest version after GitHub Pages has published it. If you have unsaved edits, you will be asked before replacing your draft.
